channel rows, message groups, composer, member rows). The pieces in the film are

exact interface; nothing is redrawn.

## Accuracy and hygiene

- Real captures only; fictional sample people and messages from the preview's
  mock data. No accounts, credentials, tokens or private conversations.
- In the capture browser only: the sample bot "Dyno" (a real third-party bot)
  reads "Helper"; a real song in a listening status reads as a made-up track;
  sample messages that can only render as error or endless loading cards in the
  preview (a relative-path video; two link previews, one to a third-party site)
  are hidden; What's New is dismissed; the Next.js dev badge is hidden.
- No Discord name, logo or asset; no claim of affiliation. Official Disband mark
  (`assets/logo/disband-mark.svg`) and slogan used exactly. No claims about
  encryption, security, numbers or users.
- Palette from the app's own tokens (see `ART-DIRECTION.md`); the output pass
  drops the tone curve's toe so the captured greys stay exact.
- Score and effects are generated by `tools/audio_morph.py`, so they are cleared
  for paid promotion. Mastered to about −14 LUFS, −1.2 dBTP.
