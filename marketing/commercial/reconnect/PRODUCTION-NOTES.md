# "The Internet, Reconnected." — production notes (internal)

38 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, for TikTok and TikTok Promote. Built in Three.js on the
pipeline's offline renderer (`src/engine3d.js`: every frame averages 8–16
jittered samples, which gives antialiasing, real depth of field and motion
blur), from the asset library in `library/` (see `library/LIBRARY.md`).

## The visual system

Everything is one continuous scene (`world.js`). World units are CSS px / 100
of the app; every part sits at the rectangle measured from the live DOM, so
whenever the parts are home the world *is* the interface — the authentic app
is never a separate screenshot laid on top.

1. **Lines** — the layout's dividers as square-section metal bars rebuilt every
   sample (per-vertex geometry). They link the tokens, snap onto the window's
   dividers and run out into an architectural grid, sweep the panels out, run
   back in depth as rails for the parts, run across the space from the
   channel rows, and finally bend into the outline of the official mark
   (sampled from the SVG's four subpaths).
2. **Tokens** — the rail's icons as extruded coins (circle, or the active
   space's squircle) with the captured icon on the face. They unstack behind
   the first one, spiral down the rail's axis, close into the rail, pop out and
   turn, fly to their channel list, and fill the mark.
3. **Strata** — channel rows, message bands, member rows, headers and composer
   as slabs with real crops on their faces: they wait in depth along their
   rails and fly forward into place, lift into louvers, ripple, and separate
   into an exploded view that closes as the camera pulls back.
4. **Type** — 3D letters (a front face plus graded layers in the app's greys),
   alpha-tested so they antialias, blur and occlude like everything else. Each
   word has a clipping plane, so letters emerge through a line as through a
   slot; "A FAMILIAR FEELING." hands its matching letters, travelling in depth,
   to "A NEW HOME.".
5. **The mark** — outlined by the converging lines, filled from below behind a
   bar of light, then a highlight passes over it.

## Review

A preview of 0–12 s (the abstract geometry resolving into the assembled app)
was rendered and reviewed before the full render; sequence 2 was redesigned
twice at that stage (close three-quarter views read as grey walls; a corridor
shot left the narrow vertical frame empty), arriving at the depth field with
converging rails.

## Accuracy

- Real captures only, from fictional sample data; no accounts, credentials,
  tokens or private conversations. Typing is real keyboard input into the
  preview's composer; the preview does not send, so no sending is shown.
- No Discord name, logo or asset; no claim of affiliation. Official mark and
  slogan used exactly. No claims about encryption, security, numbers or users.
- Score and effects generated by `tools/audio_reconnect.py`, cleared for paid
  promotion; about −14 LUFS, −1.2 dBTP.
