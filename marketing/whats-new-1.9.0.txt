A big update to calls, notifications and everyday chat.

CALLS, REDESIGNED
• A new full-screen call view with a blurred backdrop, a self-view you can drag into any corner and tap to enlarge, and controls that fade away until you tap.
• Minimise a call and keep using Disband — servers, DMs and notes stay available while you talk.
• Swipe to answer. Drag right to pick up, left to decline, with either end still tappable.
• Answering on one device now stops your other devices ringing.
• Calls connect through a relay, so they work on networks that previously couldn't establish a connection.

NOTIFICATIONS
• Push notifications now arrive for direct messages, group chats, mentions and friend requests — including when Disband is closed.
• No banner for the conversation you already have open.

CHAT
• Server invites appear as a card you can join from, instead of a plain link.
• Links show a preview with the page title and image.
• GIFs play instead of showing a single frozen frame.
• Emoji-only messages render large.
• More space where a new person starts talking, so a conversation is easier to follow.
• A bigger message box with the attachment button built into it.

FRIENDS AND PROFILE
• Search your friends list and sort it A–Z or by when you added someone, with anyone new pinned to the top.
• Change your username from the app.
• Redesigned server settings with a live preview of your icon, banner and name.
• Unread conversations are marked and move to the top of the list.

FIXES
• Signing in no longer leaves an account looking empty — sessions are stored reliably and recovered rather than silently dropped.
• Confirmation emails land on a page that tells you the address was verified.
• Deleting a note removes it everywhere, on the phone and on the web.
