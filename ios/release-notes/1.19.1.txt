What's new in Disband 1.19.1

• No more surprise sign-outs. Some of you were being signed out at random, often after the app had been in the background or on a patchy connection. You now stay signed in, and if Disband can't connect it says so and catches up by itself once you're back online.

In case you missed 1.19:
• Unread spaces and channels: a small white dot beside a space shows it has new messages, and unread channels stand out in bold.
• Read once, read everywhere: catch up on your computer and your iPhone clears too, and the other way round.
• New messages arrive live again after the app has been in the background.
• Chats stay at the newest message as photos, GIFs and links load.
• Role pings show in the role's own colour, and game activity cards show the game's artwork.
• Plus other fixes and improvements.
