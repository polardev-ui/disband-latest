What's new in Disband 1.20

• Forums: spaces can now have forum channels. Browse posts, pinned ones first, and start your own with a title and a message.
• Bot commands: type / in a channel to see the commands your space's bots offer, fill in the options and send.
• Bot replies show who ran the command, and bot buttons work right in the chat.
• Bots show as online while they're running, so you can tell at a glance when one is up.
• Create forum channels from your space's settings.
• Fixed spaces with a forum channel showing no channels at all.
• Plus other fixes and improvements.
