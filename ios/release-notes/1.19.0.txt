What's new in Disband 1.19

• Unread spaces and channels: a small white dot beside a space shows it has new messages, and unread channels stand out in bold.
• Read once, read everywhere: catch up on your computer and your iPhone clears too, and the other way round.
• New messages arrive live again after the app has been in the background.
• Chats stay at the newest message as photos, GIFs and links load, and pictures keep their size instead of jumping when they appear.
• Role pings now show in the role's own colour.
• Your status follows you: set Online on your computer and you're Online here too the next time you open the app.
• Game activity cards show the game's artwork.
• Plus other fixes and improvements.
