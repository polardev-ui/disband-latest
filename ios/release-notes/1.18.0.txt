What's new in Disband 1.18

• Disband on iPad: a native app that works in any orientation, with Split View and Stage Manager. Your spaces and channels stay on the left while the conversation opens beside them.
• Polls: vote with a tap and watch the results update live. Create your own from the + menu in any chat, with up to 10 answers.
• Show or hide your badges: go to You > Badges to choose which ones appear beside your name.
• Your guild tag and badges now sit together in one tidy row on profiles.
• Photos from your iPhone now show for everyone, including people on Android, Windows and the web.
• Channel names keep their spaces and symbols, and you can still link them, e.g. #hall-of-fame.
• Disband Staff can now moderate from the app.
• Plus other fixes and improvements.
