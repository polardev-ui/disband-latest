October update

Disband has changed a lot. Come and see.

If you haven’t opened Disband in a little while, it’s worth another look. In the last few weeks we’ve rebuilt the desktop app, launched the Creator Program, added games you can play with your friends, and brought a lot more to iPhone and Android.

Here’s everything that’s new.
Open Disband
 

Desktop & web

A whole new look

The desktop and web app have been reskinned from top to bottom: redesigned profiles and badges, a new Discover page, a cleaner My Account and a new sign-in screen. It starts faster too, so you’re in your spaces in moments.

 
 Search that works — a search bar in the header that actually works, with icons that finally line up
 
 Rich embeds — links, GitHub pushes and webhooks show as proper cards
 
 Replies and timestamps — Discord-style times, a new-messages card, and a note when the original message was deleted
 
 Calls — faster to connect, with noise suppression built in
 

New · Creator Program

Make videos or stream? Get the Content Creator badge.

Connect your YouTube, TikTok or Twitch account in Settings → Creator Program. Once you pass 2,500 followers on any of them, the Content Creator badge appears on your profile automatically.

 
 Your count on hover — anyone who hovers your badge sees your follower count
 
 Private by design — we read your follower count once and keep only the number and your handle, never your login
 
 Get discovered — creators can promote their spaces on Discover
 

Games

Play together, right in the chat

Start a game in any channel or DM and everyone can join. Five new games have just landed — Hangman, Emoji Riddles, Word Scramble, Minesweeper and Higher or Lower — alongside Wordle, Trivia, Four in a Row, Reversi and more.

Games stay open for 24 hours, close as soon as everyone has finished, and keep their results so you can see who won.

Show what you’re playing

The desktop app now picks up the game you’re playing — Roblox, Fortnite, Steam games and thousands more — and shows it on your profile with the game’s logo and how long you’ve been playing.
 

Make it yours

Glimmers, decorations and gifts

 
 Glimmers — our new currency, for decorations and more in the shop
 
 Avatar decorations — halos, horns, a race car doing laps and more, now on mobile too
 
 Name styles — new fonts and gradients for your display name
 
 Gifting — send a decoration to a friend, paid for by card or with Glimmers
 
 Catalysts — boost the spaces you love, and move one to another space after its first week
 

iPhone & Android

Mobile caught up

Disband on iPhone and Android now has rich embeds, Discord-style chat, Discover, Space Settings, guild tags, decorations and partner badges. Typing and scrolling feel much smoother, and DMs open at your latest message.
 

And there’s more

 
 Connections — show your music on your profile, including YouTube Music through Last.fm
 
 Passkeys — use a passkey as your second sign-in step
 
 Self-assign roles — pick your own roles in spaces that offer them
 
 Sign in with Disband — use your Disband account on other apps and sites
 
 Developers — a new developer portal for bots, webhooks and game activity
 
 Status page — check how Disband is doing at any time
